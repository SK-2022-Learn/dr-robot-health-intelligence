# Dr. Robot v1 — five-minute demo

Use only the fictional seeded profiles and synthetic report in `data/`. Keep the API and web app visible in separate terminals.

## 0:00 — Problem and dashboard

Open Home, select the fictional Self profile, and explain that SQLite is authoritative health memory while every fact remains traceable to a source.

## 0:40 — Upload and extraction review

Open Uploads and upload a synthetic PDF or TXT report. Show parsed text and confirm My Health did not change. Choose **Extract Health Facts**, then accept one candidate, correct one, and reject one. Only accepted/corrected records should appear in My Health.

## 2:00 — Daily Chat

Open Ask Dr. Robot and enter: “Morning sugar 118 before food. After breakfast 172. Slept 6 hours. Little constipation. Walked 25 minutes.” Show the structured preview and confirm it. Then enter “Sugar 165”; show that clarification is required and nothing is saved before clarification and confirmation.

## 2:40 — Timeline and WHY

Open Timeline. Show document-backed and chat-backed records, then open **WHY** to display the exact document page or immutable source message.

## 3:10 — What Changed?

Open Insights, run What Changed, and open WHY. Point out the exact recent/baseline windows, values, formula, data-confidence explanation, and evidence IDs.

## 3:40 — Family

Open Family. Show relationships and repeated conditions. Confirm the private sibling remains “Private profile” and contributes no detail or evidence.

## 4:10 — Safety

Ask “Should I stop metformin?” and show the medication boundary. Ask “I have crushing chest pain and cannot breathe.” and show the urgent escalation with no normal graph nodes after `safety_pre`.

## 4:30 — Doctor Visit

Ask “Prepare my doctor visit summary,” then open the complete Doctor Visit screen. Show history, recent changes, medications, labs, symptoms, gaps, questions, evidence, and Print / Save as PDF.

## 4:50 — Closing

Ask “What changed with my post-meal glucose?”, “Why do you say my HbA1c was 7.2%?”, and “Which conditions repeat in my family?” Expand WHY to show the service actions and sources. Close by noting that Dr. Robot organizes and explains recorded information; it does not diagnose or prescribe.
